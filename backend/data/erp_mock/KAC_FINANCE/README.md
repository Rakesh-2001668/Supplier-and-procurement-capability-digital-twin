# KAC_FINANCE: MOCK finance datasets of tenant KAC (ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE)

**Everything here is mock data.** Kaveri Auto Components is a fictional prospect, the bank statements are generated, the rates are not RBI data, the account numbers
are obviously fake (`MOCK-CA-0001`, IFSC `MOCK0000001`) and nothing in this folder is the customer's bank or tax record. It exists so the demo tenant shows a working
accounts-payable function before the customer's real data arrives. The customer replaces it with its own (see "What the customer supplies for real").

## How the pack builds it (all through the real services, the business clock pinned to each document's date)

Dates: 1 September to 31 October 2026 (the KAC cutover key date is 31 October; the loaded open invoices are dated January to November 2026, 96 % in September and
October, and the purchase orders in October). One company code (1000). Events are sorted by date and executed one transaction each, so a credit note dated 20 October is
never netted into a payment of 2 October. Goods are received into storage location 0002 (receiving area, created for plants 1200 and 1300; inventory_ops made it for 1100), so the opening-stock balances in 0001 stay as the inventory datasets left them; materials at or below their reorder point are not received into. The pack creates about 500 service-driven documents for KAC (goods receipts, invoices, payments, notes, offers, files, statements, advances).

### 2. Exceptions and notes

34 invoice exceptions: DUPLICATE: open 2, resolved (not duplicate) 2, resolved (rejected) 2; MISSING_GR: open 2, resolved (gr posted) 4; PRICE: open 4, open (dispute) 1, open (price approval pending) 1, resolved (credit note received) 3, resolved (price accepted) 2; QUANTITY: open 2, resolved (credit note received) 5, resolved (rejected) 1; TAX: open 1, resolved (rejected) 2.
"In resolution" means a dispute raised with the supplier, or a price acceptance waiting for the buyer. 5 invoices are REJECTED with reasons.
Credit notes: 20 (price, quantity, quality and return reasons; 5 resolve exceptions, 15 are against posted invoices and went to the ERP as credit memos; a credit note has no draft /
approved state in PIC: its states are those of the invoice and of the ERP memo). Debit notes: APPROVED 1, MATCHED 2, PENDING_APPROVAL 3, POSTED 3, REJECTED 3 (RECEIVED is only a transient state: a debit note is matched or referred at once).

### 3. Tax data

TDS sections on suppliers: 194C-IND 5, 194C-OTH 72, 194I-LB 2, 194J-PRO 13, 194J-TECH 11, 194Q 1. PAN comes from the GSTIN for all domestic suppliers. s.197 certificates: LDC-MOCK-2627-0001 1.0 % to 2027-03-31; LDC-MOCK-2627-0002 1.0 % to 2026-11-15; LDC-MOCK-2627-0003 0.5 % to 2027-03-31 (cap INR 2,500,000); LDC-MOCK-2627-0004 1.0 % to 2027-05-31.
E-invoice: the GST invoices of the pack carry an IRN = SHA-256(supplier GSTIN + financial year + "INV" + document number) and a signed QR payload (JWT-shaped, standard fields, MOCK signature);
52 verify as valid and 2 are invalid on purpose (IRN built for another document number; QR total INR 50,000 above the invoice); one more invoice carries a valid IRN with a GST rate
(12 %) that differs from the order (18 %).

### 4. FX

765 rows in the tenant (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY to INR, every weekday 1 April - 30 October 2026). Formula (see `app/pic/tools/finance_mock.py`): anchor x raw(x) / raw(0) x (1 + noise), x = days from
28 October 2026, raw(x) = 1 + 0.018 sin(2 pi x / 97 + phase) + 0.007 sin(2 pi x / 31 + 1.7 phase) + drift x, noise +-0.12 % from SHA-256(currency|date); anchors are the FBIL values of 28 October
(USD 95.2278, EUR 105.8641, GBP 126.4657, JPY 0.6031 per yen; CNY = USD / 7.25); flat from 29 October. Foreign-currency invoices (MOCK import vendors 900001-900003): EUR PAID/PAID 1, EUR POSTED/UNPAID 1, JPY POSTED/UNPAID 1, USD PAID/PAID 2; the rate is fixed at
the first posting; the foreign payments are in their own currency, in a run of their own. The platform's GST check has no import mode, so the import orders carry 18 % (IGST) on the line.

## Deliberately wrong in the data (so the controls have something to show)

Invoices with price +3 % to +6 % over the order; quantity +10 % to +20 % over the goods received; invoices before the goods receipt (missing GR); an invalid IRN, an invalid QR total, a wrong GST rate;
a duplicate re-billed with the same amount and date (rejected), a duplicate held by number variant (`FO26/x` / `FO26-x`, released as "not a duplicate"); a pain.001 file rejected by the bank; a bulk bank
line that does not add up; an unmatched customer receipt; payments the bank returned; MSME payments after the due date; a certificate that expires on 15 November 2026; invoices of blocked suppliers that
stay payment-blocked and unpaid (old, overdue, 90+ days); debit notes waiting for the buyer; a price acceptance waiting for the buyer; a dispute.

## What the customer supplies for real

Bank statements and payment-file specification (and whether the bank echoes the payment number), debtor accounts, the RBI bank rate it uses for MSME interest, PAN / TDS section / Udyam per supplier and
its TAN, s.197 certificates (with amount caps), the tax team's sections and thresholds, the FX source (FBIL or a licensed feed), the fiscal calendar and period-close authorities, approval bands and limits,
IRP / GSP credentials, the ERP posting profile for TDS, MSME interest, credit memos and payment reversal. See `docs/PIC_Finance_Rule_Values_Proposal.md`.
