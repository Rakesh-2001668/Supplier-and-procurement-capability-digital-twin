# KAC_INVENTORY: MOCK opening stock of tenant KAC (ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE)

**Everything here is mock data.** Kaveri Auto Components is a fictional prospect, the quantities are generated, and nothing in this folder is the
customer's stock list. It exists so the demo tenant behaves like a configured production tenant before the customer's real opening stock arrives.
The customer replaces it with its own file (see "Loading a real file").

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `opening_stock.csv` | 1,624 opening-stock lines of 1,057 stock materials at plants 1100 (599 lines), 1200 (577), 1300 (448), as of 31 October 2026 (the SAP cutover key date), in the layout of `app.pic.tools.stock_opening_load` |
| `MANIFEST.json` | row count, SHA-256, label and the counts of the deliberate exceptions below |
| `README.md` | this file |

Generated by `python -m app.pic.tools.inventory_mock` from the KAC cutover extract (`data/erp_mock/KAC`); the same inputs always give the same bytes
(the test compares the SHA-256). Loaded by the dataset pack `inventory_master` (D14) with the loader `python -m app.pic.tools.stock_opening_load`.

## Columns

`material, plant, storage_location, stock_type, batch, qty, uom, expiry, valuation`: material number as loaded from SAP, plant, storage location (always
`0001`), stock type (`UNRESTRICTED`, `QUALITY`, `BLOCKED`, `CONSIGNMENT`), batch (only for batch-managed materials), quantity in the base unit, unit,
batch expiry date (ISO) and the value of the line in INR (`qty x standard price`, to the paisa, so the file reconciles to the ledger exactly).

## How the quantities were derived (all formulas are in `tools/inventory_mock.py`; nothing is random, every choice is a SHA-256 of material and plant)

1. **Usage per month** of a material at a plant, first source that exists: the quantity bought in the last 12 months before 31 October 2026 divided by 12
   (else the last 24 months divided by 24), from the purchase-order history of the SAP extract (EKPO x EKKO); 1,365 of 1,554 material-plants have it. The other
   189 use one typical lot a month (fixed lot, else minimum lot, else INR 50,000 divided by the standard price). Every usage has a floor of one unit a month.
2. **Cover** = 20 + 40 x u days, u in [0, 1) from the hash of `material|plant`. **Quantity** = the larger of `ceil(daily usage x cover)` and
   `ceil(1.05 x reorder point)` where the reorder point is `daily usage x lead time + safety stock` (the formulas of the master-data fill). The second term is
   capped at INR 25 lakh of stock per line: 34 lines (die sets of INR 35 lakh each, which the SAP master gives a safety stock of 34 pieces) are therefore
   stocked at a few pieces and sit below that safety stock. That is a data-quality finding for the customer's master-data review, not a stocking decision.
3. **Result**: about 68 % of the lines hold 20 to 60 days of cover, about 1 % less than 20 (the deliberate low lines), about 31 % more than 60 (slow movers
   with a one-piece minimum and materials whose SAP safety stock is larger than two months of usage). Total value INR 50.2 crore at standard price.
   The tests assert at least 60 % in the 20 to 60 day range.

## Master-data formulas of the `inventory_master` pack (they fill only what is missing; a value that is set is never overwritten)

Quantities are rounded up to whole units, a month is 30 days. Usage per month is the one above (history, else on hand / 1.5 for a tenant without purchase history,
else one typical lot, floor one unit).

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Standard price (only where 0 or empty) | price of the material's latest purchase-order line, else its latest contract price period, else its latest info-record price; no source: left empty (nothing is invented). All 1,068 KAC stock materials already carry one from SAP. |
| Safety stock (only where 0 or empty) | days of average usage by material category x average daily usage over the material's plants: die casting, ferrous castings, forgings, machining, intercompany 7 days; raw materials, rubber and seals, electronics, packaging 10; fasteners, MRO, lubricants, chemicals, tooling, office and safety 14 (7 for any other); without usable usage 20 % of the typical lot, at least 1. 147 KAC materials had none. |
| Reorder point per plant | daily usage of the plant x lead time + safety stock; lead time = planned delivery days + goods-receipt processing days (7 days when both are 0) |
| Maximum stock per plant | reorder point + lot size; lot size = fixed lot, else minimum lot, else pack size, else one month of usage (at least 1). Where a reorder point exists and the maximum is empty only the maximum is filled. |
| Batch / expiry flags (keys not yet in the attributes) | chemicals: batch + expiry, minimum shelf life 90 days; lubricants: batch + expiry, 180; natural rubber and EPDM compounds: batch + expiry, 60; steel, aluminium and polymer raw-material lots: batch only; adhesive tapes and labels: batch + expiry, 90. Not on a material with an open purchase-order line or with batch-less stock. |

Every filled material gets one audit event `MASTER_DATA_FILLED` (before and after, reason "MOCK sample data ...") and the attribute `mockFilled`; every reorder-point row carries a
note that starts with "MOCK sample data". The three signature-case materials of tenant AXN are never touched.

## Deliberate exceptions (so the alerts, KPIs and approvals have something to show)

| Exception | Lines | How they look |
|---|---|---|
| Stock-outs | 3 | quantity `0` (nothing is posted; the loader lists them): 23000083 at 1100, 25000005 at 1200, 25000042 at 1100 |
| Below safety stock | 12 | half of the safety stock |
| Between safety stock and reorder point | 8 | safety stock plus half of the lead-time demand |
| Expiring in about 30 / 60 / 90 days | 2 / 2 / 2 batches | batch `L2609A` of six expiry-managed materials, expiry 21-27 / 44-58 / 72-88 days after 31 October 2026 (the mock is "as of" that date: viewed months later these lots show as expired) |
| Quality-hold lots | 3 | stock type `QUALITY`, about 5 days of usage, on quality-managed materials |
| Blocked lots | 3 | stock type `BLOCKED`, about 3 days of usage |
| Consignment | 1 | 23000001 at 1300, supplier-owned stock, about 20 days of usage |

Batch-managed materials (chemicals, lubricants, rubber compounds, raw-material lots, adhesive tapes and labels; 48 materials, 37 of them expiry managed)
carry batches `L2609A` / `L2610B` (expiry managed, 60 % / 40 %, one expiry per batch) or `L2610A`. A material with an open purchase-order line, or one of the 20
materials the `inventory_ops` pack opens at plant 1100, is not switched to batch management and has no batch in the file. Those 20 balances are left out of the file.

## Loading a real file (the production path)

```
python -m app.pic.tools.stock_opening_load --tenant KAC --file <the client's opening_stock.csv> --dry-run     # validate and reconcile, writes nothing
python -m app.pic.tools.stock_opening_load --tenant KAC --file <the client's opening_stock.csv>               # post (movement 561)
```

The customer exports its ERP stock list (SAP MARD, MCHB, MKOL and MSKA with the batch master MCH1 and the valuation MBEW), maps it to the columns above, and
reconciles the `valuation` column with the report. The file layout, the validation errors and the reconciliation are in `docs/runbooks/inventory_operations.md`
section 10. The mock opening stock of tenant KAC must not be loaded into a tenant that has its own: use a different tenant code or skip the pack.
