# KAC_SOURCING: MOCK strategic-sourcing datasets (pack `sourcing_ops`)

**Everything here is MOCK / ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE.** The prices, indices, benchmarks, twin figures, supplier terms and scores are generated from SHA-256 of
keys; they are not market data and not any supplier's quotation. The customer replaces them with its own data and signs off the rule values
(SOURCING-POLICY, SOURCING-TCO) before go-live. Every title, reason and rule description of the pack carries `MOCK`.

The pack `sourcing_ops` (key `sourcing-ops`, version 1, `backend/app/pic/seed/datasets/sourcing_ops*.py`) requires `finance-ops` and loads into **KAC**
(Kaveri Auto Components, the first client) and **AXN** (the signature-case tenant). It builds the documents through the real services (requisition,
sourcing, auction, sourcing_templates, should_cost, contract) as named users with the business clock pinned to each step's date, so state machines,
SoD, ABAC, sealed bids, audit hash chains, outbox events, contract drafts, savings initiatives and notifications behave as in production.

## Formulas and rules

* **Index series**: `value(m) = 100 x (1 + 0.035 sin(2 pi m / 11 + phase) + drift x m) x (1 + noise)`, m = months from April 2025, noise +-0.4 % from SHA-256.
* **Should-cost** (rupees per unit): `material = qty x (1 + 5 % yield loss) x rate x index(on) / index(1 April 2025)`; `conversion = minutes x rate per minute`; `tooling`,
  `packaging` fixed; `logistics = 2 % of (material + conversion)`; `unit cost = subtotal x (1 + margin %)`. The pack scales the quantities, minutes and fixed amounts of an
  event so that the unit cost is 93 % of the item's last price.
* **Bid** of supplier s on item i: `price = baseline x (1 + spread)`, spread from SHA-256 of (event, line, supplier) between -11 % and +9 %; freight 0.8-3 % and packaging
  0.4-1.5 % of the price (about one bidder in four quotes EXW with no freight, so the TCO rule estimates it from the incoterm); payment terms 30-90 days; lead time 7-45 days; capacity
  1.15-2.4 x the monthly quantity; offer valid 45 days after the close. The baseline is the item's `last_price_minor`, a 4 % higher budget, the weighted average of the last
  12 months of orders (events from 24 September 2026, when KAC's orders exist), the market quote or the should-cost, as the event's method says.
* **Dates**: an event is created at 10:00 IST, published 10:30; RFQ bids close 7 days later at 17:00 (RFP and RFI 10 days, auction 3 days at 15:00, open events 14 days,
  published events 26 days). The close is made by the pack at close + 2 minutes exactly as the timer does; award approvals one day after the proposal.
* **TCO**: the SOURCING-TCO rule (`carryingRatePct` 17, `costOfCapitalPct` 11.5, `copqPerDefectMinor` 400,000, freight estimates by incoterm, `dutyRates` by HS prefix: 72 -> 7.5 %,
  7616 -> 10 %, 3926 -> 10 %, 85 -> 10 %, 40 -> 10 %, 73 -> 7.5 %) frozen in each event; PPM from the twin snapshot.

## Deliberately wrong or awkward cases (they exist to show a control working)

| case | where | what the system does |
|---|---|---|
| bid after the close | K03 | refused 409 `EVENT_CLOSED`, audit `LATE_BID_REFUSED` |
| bid by a supplier that was not invited | K03 | refused 403 `NOT_INVITED`, audit `UNINVITED_BID_REFUSED` |
| bid withdrawn and submitted again; bid replaced before the close | K03 | audit `BID_WITHDRAWN`, `BID_REPLACED` |
| the sealed bids viewed before the opening | K24 (waiting for the opening), K27 (open) | refused 423 `BIDS_SEALED`, audit `BID_ACCESS_DENIED` |
| two-person opening | K15 (two openers, evaluated), K25 (one opener, still closed), K24 (none) | the bids stay sealed until two different people have opened them |
| import bids in USD, one with an HS code that has no duty rate | K11 | converted at the rate of the event's reference date; the line with no duty rate has `tco_incomplete` and is not zero-filled |
| failed technical gate and a disqualified bidder | K10 | the gate-failing bidder is left out of every scenario; the disqualification keeps the bid on record with its reason |
| award to a non-lowest bid | K05 (awarded), K18 (pending), K36 | a written rationale of at least 15 characters is on the decision; approvals follow; some also carry `NEGATIVE_SAVINGS` (the award costs more than the baseline) |
| limited competition | K39 | two valid bids against a minimum of three: `competitive` false, `LIMITED_COMPETITION` exception, rationale and approval |
| capacity-constrained award | K06 | the lowest bidder offers 55 % of the volume: the optimiser awards a dual source |
| auction soft close | K12 | two bids inside the trigger window extend the end twice (`AUCTION_EXTENDED`) |
| award withdrawn by cancellation; cancellation after the bids; cancellation while open; draft withdrawn | K36, K35, K37, K34 | the event is CANCELLED, tasks and timers are closed, requirement lines go back to OPEN, savings initiatives are LOST |

## How the pack runs

* Idempotent: each step writes an audit marker `SOURCING_OPS_STAGE` (object_ref `K03:bid-100062`) in its own transaction and returns the state later steps need; a second run
  finds the markers, calls no service and writes nothing. An interrupted run resumes.
* **No scheduler is run.** The pack never calls `worker.tick()` / `Scenario.relay()` (those fire every tenant's timers and scheduled jobs, including the jobs that move contract
  statuses). Consumers are delivered with `outbox.relay_tenant()`; a close is made by calling `sourcing.close(by_timer=True)` as the timer does and the timers of events the
  pack closed or cancelled are marked fired. The close/open timers of the events that are still OPEN or PUBLISHED stay and fall due after 4 November 2026.
* Not touched: KAC contracts `KAC-*`, invoices, payments, inventory and spend rows (the test hashes them).
* Volume: 604 service-driven calls for KAC (requisitions, events, bids, scoring, awards, approvals, contracts) and 76 for AXN; about 140-225 s for KAC and 25 s for AXN on a
  local PostgreSQL. Over a high-latency link expect it to be proportional to the number of calls (the inventory pack took about an hour for 1,600 postings).
* Run: `datasets.apply_pack("sourcing_ops", password)` (the Neon setup tool applies all ready packs in order); verify with
  `python -m app.pic.tools.sourcing_mock --check` and `pytest tests/pic/test_datasets_sourcing_ops.py`.
